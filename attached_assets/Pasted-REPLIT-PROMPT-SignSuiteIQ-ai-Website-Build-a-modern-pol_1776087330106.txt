REPLIT PROMPT — SignSuiteIQ.ai Website

Build a modern, polished marketing website for SignSuiteIQ.ai — a SaaS platform for the sign industry comprising three AI-powered applications: InstalliQ.ai, SignSalesIQ, and SignTakeoffIQ. Users can subscribe to one app or the full suite.

TECH STACK

Next.js 14 (App Router)
Tailwind CSS
Framer Motion for animations
shadcn/ui for UI components
React Icons
Deploy-ready on Replit


BRAND & DESIGN SYSTEM
Colors:

Navy dark: #0B1E3D
Navy mid: #1B3A6B
Cyan accent: #29ABE2
Light cyan: #7DD3F0
White: #FFFFFF
Off-white surface: #F4F8FD
Body text: #1A1A2E
Muted text: #64748B

Typography:

Font: Inter (Google Fonts)
Headings: 500 weight, tight tracking
Body: 400 weight, 1.7 line height
All UI labels: uppercase, wide letter-spacing

Design language:

Flat, clean surfaces — no mesh gradients or noise textures
Subtle 0.5px borders on cards
Generous whitespace
Sticky nav with blur backdrop
Smooth scroll between sections
Framer Motion fade-up animations on scroll entry for all sections and cards
Mobile-first, fully responsive


SITE STRUCTURE — BUILD ALL PAGES
1. Homepage (/)

Sticky nav: logo left, links center (Products, Pricing, How It Works, About), "Start Free Trial" CTA button right. Blur backdrop on scroll.
Hero section: dark navy gradient background, large headline "One suite. Three powerful tools.", subheadline about AI-powered sign industry software, two CTAs — "Start Free Trial" (cyan filled) and "See All Products" (outlined). Animated floating graphic or abstract IQ icon mark in the background.
Three app cards section: one card per app, each with its badge color (InstalliQ = teal, SignSalesIQ = blue, SignTakeoffIQ = amber), app name, tagline, 4 feature bullets, and "Learn more" link.
Suite value band: dark navy background, headline "Built for sign professionals end to end", six feature pills (ADA & fire code grounded, Real-time customer comms, AI plan recognition, 47+ mock-up types, Cloud-based anywhere access, No per-seat pricing surprises).
Pricing section: 4 plan cards — InstalliQ ($99/mo), SignSalesIQ ($99/mo), SignTakeoffIQ ($99/mo), Full Suite ($249/mo). Full Suite card highlighted with cyan border and "Best Value" badge. Each card has feature list and CTA button.
How it works: 4-step horizontal flow — Upload plans → AI processing → Review & edit → Send to customers. Each step numbered and animated.
Testimonial band (placeholder): 3 quote cards with dummy sign company names and roles.
Final CTA band: navy gradient, "Ready to visualize the win?", "Start your free trial today" button.
Footer: logo, app links, nav links, copyright.


2. InstalliQ.ai Product Page (/installiiq)
Hero: teal accent, headline "AI-Powered Field Proof", subheadline about installation workflow.
Feature sections (alternating image-left / text-right layout):

Built-in calendar & weather: schedule crews, check conditions before dispatch
AI work order recognition: upload PDF/image/email, AI parses job details automatically
Photo capture & tagging: structured before/during/after capture, AI auto-tags by sign and location
Site survey tools: document site conditions pre-installation
Customer communication: email or download polished field proof packages directly to clients

Include a "How a job flows through InstalliQ" animated step diagram.
Bottom CTA: "Start your free trial of InstalliQ.ai"

3. SignSalesIQ Product Page (/signsalesiq)
Hero: blue accent, headline "Close Deals Faster", subheadline about AI mock-ups in minutes not days.
Feature sections:

47+ sign type mock-ups: channel letters, monument, pylon, ADA, vehicle wraps, window graphics, blade signs, dimensional letters, LED displays, wayfinding, and more
Real environment placement: upload building photo, AI places mock-up on the real location
Real-time adjustments: colors, fonts, sizing — no designer needed
Client presentation tools: branded PDF proposals, side-by-side variations, before/after toggle
Quote-to-close workflow: built-in pricing calculator, proposal approval, e-signature

Include a "Minutes to mock-up" speed comparison graphic (Traditional: 3-5 days vs SignSalesIQ: minutes).
Bottom CTA: "Start your free trial of SignSalesIQ"

4. SignTakeoffIQ Product Page (/signtakeoffiq)
Hero: amber accent, headline "Takeoffs in Minutes, Not Hours", subheadline about AI-driven plan reading with code compliance built in.
Feature sections:

AI plan recognition: reads PDF, image, or CAD exports — identifies all sign locations automatically
Code compliance engine: 2010 ADA Standards (Sections 216 & 703) + local fire code, jurisdiction-aware rule sets, flags missing or non-compliant locations
Automated read count: full sign schedule by type, location, floor, and code category
Marked-up plan output: editable plans with sign locations clearly marked and labeled
Editable sign schedule: adjust quantities and types, auto-updating schedule, export as PDF or spreadsheet

Include a before/after plan view mockup graphic showing a raw plan vs a marked-up plan.
Bottom CTA: "Start your free trial of SignTakeoffIQ"

ANIMATIONS (Framer Motion)

All sections fade up on scroll entry (opacity: 0 → 1, y: 30 → 0, duration: 0.5)
App cards stagger in with 0.1s delay between each
Pricing cards lift slightly on hover (y: -4)
Nav CTA button pulses subtly on load
Step numbers count up on scroll entry
