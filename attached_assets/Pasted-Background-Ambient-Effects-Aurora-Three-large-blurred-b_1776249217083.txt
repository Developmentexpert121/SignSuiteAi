Background / Ambient Effects

Aurora — Three large blurred blobs slowly drift and shift colors in the background (orange/primary tones) using CSS @keyframes. They loop infinitely and create a soft glowing aurora effect.
Constellation Canvas — A live canvas with 30 floating dots that drift around slowly. Lines are drawn between any two dots within 100px of each other, fading based on distance. Runs continuously via requestAnimationFrame.
Floating Particles — 10 small dots float upward from the bottom of the panel with randomized sizes, speeds (5–12s), and delays, looping infinitely.
Background Orbs — Two large blurred gradient circles on the left panel that gently float up and down on separate slow cycles (8s and 10s).
Mouse Spotlight — A large soft radial gradient follows the user's cursor across the right panel (700ms eased transition), creating a subtle illumination effect.
Login Card

Scale-in Entrance — The card scales in from slightly smaller to full size on page load (0.5s, with a 0.2s delay).
Tilt on Hover — The card tilts up to ±8° on both axes as the mouse moves over it. A glare/shine gradient also follows the cursor position. Both reset smoothly on mouse leave.
Card Glow — A soft glowing ring pulses slowly around the card border via a ::before pseudo-element.
Form Fields

Staggered Entrance — Each field group slides up and fades in one after another with staggered delays (0.3s → 0.45s → 0.6s → 0.75s → 0.9s), so the form appears to "build" from top to bottom.
Input Focus Scale — When an email or password field is focused, the input wrapper scales up very slightly (scale-[1.01]) and the icon color transitions to the primary color.
Input Glow — A glowing ring/shadow appears around the focused input field.
Sign In Button

Shimmer Sweep — A white highlight sweeps diagonally across the button on a loop, giving it a glowing sheen effect.
Hover Scale / Active Press — Scales up slightly on hover (scale-[1.02]) and presses down on click (scale-[0.98]).
Arrow Icon Slide — The arrow icon inside the button slides slightly to the right on hover.
Ripple on Click — Clicking the button spawns a circular ripple that expands and fades out from the exact click position (600ms).
Left Panel — Branding / Stats

Typewriter — The headline subtitle cycles through multiple phrases, typing each one out character by character (80ms/char), pausing for 2s, then deleting it (40ms/char) and moving to the next.
Animated Counters — The stat numbers (10K+, 500+, 50K+) count up from 0 to their target on load using an ease-out cubic curve over 2 seconds.
Gradient Text — The main heading uses an animated shifting gradient that cycles through colors with a 6s loop (background-size: 200%).
Feature Item Hover — Feature list items shift slightly to the right and lighten on hover.
Stat Item Hover — Stat boxes have a subtle scale/glow on hover.
Orbs (left panel) — Three glowing orbs drift with separate timings (8s, 10s, 12s) layered behind the content.
Miscellaneous

Security Badge Check Icons — The checkmarks on the SSL/SOC2 trust badges have a small draw-on animation (SVG stroke animation).
"Or" Separator Lines — The divider lines on either side of the "or" text have a shimmer/animated gradient sweep.
Dot Bounce Loader — A 3-dot bouncing indicator (staggered 0.16s apart) appears in certain loading states.
Version Tag — Fades in when Ctrl/Cmd-clicked on the logo icon.
reduce-motion support — All of the above animations are disabled/paused when the user has prefers-reduced-motion: reduce set in their OS.
